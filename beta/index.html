Help test Guide Tones.

Guide Tones is in testing on Android and iOS. Join in, try the drills, and tell us what works and what does not.

Android

Join on Google Play

  1. Send us your Google account email.Use the address you sign in to Google Play with, so you can be added to the tester list.
    Email to join
  2. Open the opt-in link.Open it with the same Google account and tap Become a tester.
    [PLAY OPT-IN LINK]
  3. Install Guide Tones.Install it from Google Play. Please stay opted in for at least two weeks. Google asks for this before an app can be released.

iPhone and iPad

Join with TestFlight

  1. Install TestFlight.TestFlight is Apple’s free app for trying apps before release. Get it from the App Store.
  2. Open the invite link.Open it on your iPhone or iPad.
    [TESTFLIGHT LINK]
  3. Accept and install.Tap Accept, then Install, and open Guide Tones.

What to expect

Everything to try

Beta builds can have all levels unlocked, so you can try every drill and Hear it.

Things are still changing. If something looks wrong or sounds wrong, that is useful to know.

Crash reports

If the app crashes, it can send a short crash report so it can be fixed.
