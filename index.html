How it works

01

Hear it

An interval, a chord, an enclosure or a progression plays, in the instrument sound you choose.

02

Answer it

Tap the keys or the chord wheel. For enclosures, play or sing the line and Guide Tones listens. Notes can be shown for B♭, E♭ and F instruments.

03

Level it up

Four levels, streaks and ranks from Woodshedding to Changes Master keep the practice going.

The drills

  • Intervals

    Make · Identify

    Make an interval from a root, or identify what you hear. A song hint for each interval helps you anchor the sound.

  • Chords

    Extension · Quality

    Name the extension on a chord, or pick out its quality. Chords are played as voiced chords, not just stacked triads.

  • Enclosures

    Play or sing

    Hear a target note approached from above and below, then play or sing the pattern back. Guide Tones listens through your microphone.

  • Progressions

    Chord wheel

    Hear a progression, then rebuild it degree by degree on the chord wheel, from a plain ii-V-I to substitutions.

Hear it · Compare chords

Hear exactly what changes.

Pick any two chords and play them side by side. Guide Tones highlights what is different, so you can hear and see that Cm7 becomes C7 by changing a single note.
