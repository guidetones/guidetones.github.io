Contact us

Email matthewhammond@outlook.com. We usually reply within a few days.

To help us sort it out quickly, please include:

  • your app version (in the app’s store listing or your phone’s app settings)
  • your phone model and its operating system version
  • what you were doing, what you expected, and what happened instead

Privacy requests

To ask what information we hold about you, or to have it corrected or deleted, email the address above and include your App Store or Google Play order number. See your rights in the privacy policy for details.

Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. Earbop has no accounts, and we do not ask for your name or email address.

Where is my progress stored?

On your phone. We do not receive it, so it does not move to a new phone unless your phone’s own backup restores it.

Do I have to allow the microphone?

No. Some drills can listen to you sing or play your answer, but you can answer with the on-screen buttons instead. You can change the permission at any time in your phone’s settings.

How do I restore a purchase?

In the app, open Settings, then Pro, and tap “Restore Purchases”. Sign in with the same Apple or Google account you bought with.
